Championship Rules & Regulations

×

1. Scoring Modes

  • Raw Reviews Mode: Ranks reviewers based solely on the total number of PR reviews completed in the calendar month.
  • Weighted Mode: Ranks reviewers based on Total Reviews × Total Story Points reviewed in the month.

2. F1 Points Allocation

At the end of each month, reviewers receive championship points based on their rank using the Formula 1 points scale:

  • 1st: 25 pts | 2nd: 18 pts | 3rd: 15 pts | 4th: 12 pts | 5th: 10 pts
  • 6th: 8 pts | 7th: 6 pts | 8th: 4 pts | 9th: 2 pts | 10th: 1 pt

3. Tie-Breaking & Countback Rules

⏱️ Monthly Tie-Breaker (First to Review):
If two or more reviewers finish a month with the exact same monthly score (or raw review count), the tie is broken by time-to-first-review. The reviewer who recorded their first review of that month earlier in time earns the higher position.

If monthly scores and timestamps are completely identical, points for those positions are split evenly between tied participants.

4. Overall Championship Standings Tie-Breaker

If reviewers are level on total championship points at any stage in the season, ties are resolved using standard F1 countback criteria:

  1. Most 1st-place finishes (Wins / 25 pts)
  2. Most Podium finishes (Top 3 placements)
  3. Most 2nd-place finishes, 3rd-place finishes, and so on.

5. Provisional Scores

The active/current month (e.g., September) displays with a dashed outline and reduced opacity to indicate that scores are provisional until the month closes.
